Signaling Game: Mutual Information Explorer

Set a prior over states T and a signaling strategy P(M|T). The joint distribution and all entropies update in real time.

Variable dimensions

Prior over T (unnormalized weights)

Signaling strategy P(M | T) — row-stochastic weights

Joint probability table P(T, M)

Entropies

H(T) — marginal entropy of T
H(M) — marginal entropy of M
H(T, M) — joint entropy
H(T | M)— conditional entropy of T given M
H(M | T)— conditional entropy of M given T
I(T ; M)— mutual information
